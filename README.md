# Vehicles-analysis-dashboard
Project Overview :-

This project is a Tableau dashboard that provides insights into electric vehicle (EV) data, including total EV counts, trends over model years, state-wise distributions, top vehicle manufacturers, and CAFV (Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle) eligibility. The visualization aims to help stakeholders understand the adoption and distribution of EVs across different regions and models.

Dataset Used :-

The dataset includes details on electric vehicles, such as:

1.Vehicle type (BEV - Battery Electric Vehicles, PHEV - Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles)

2.Model year

3.Manufacturer

4.State-wise distribution

5.CAFV eligibility

6.Electric range

Dashboard Features :-

The Tableau dashboard consists of multiple visual components:
1.Key Metrics Summary :-

-> Total EVs: 1,50,437

->Average Electric Range: 67.86 miles

->BEV Count: 1,16,766 (77.62%)

->PHEV Count: 33,671 (22.38%)

2.Total Vehicles by Model Year :-

->Displays the trend of EV adoption over time.

->Shows the increase in EV registrations, peaking in recent years.

3.Total Vehicles by State :-

->A geographic map visualization of EV distribution across the United States.

->States with the highest EV adoption are highlighted.

4.Top 10 Vehicles by Manufacturer :-

->Identifies leading EV manufacturers based on the number of vehicles registered.

->Tesla dominates with 52.71% of total vehicles.

5.Total Vehicles by CAFV Eligibility :-

->A pie chart showing the percentage of CAFV-eligible, non-eligible, and unknown vehicles.

->Total Vehicles by Model.

->Displays the most popular EV models along with their contribution to the total EV count.

6.Top models include :-

->Tesla Model Y (18.95%)

->Tesla Model 3 (18.42%)

->Nissan LEAF (8.76%)

Tools Used :-

1.Tableau: For data visualization and dashboard creation.

2.Excel: Data cleaning and preparation.

3.Tableau Calculated Fields: Used for performing metric calculations such as total vehicles, percentage distributions, and filtering data dynamically.

Insights & Conclusion :-

->Tesla is the leading EV manufacturer, contributing the most vehicles.

->EV adoption has grown significantly since 2010, with a sharp increase in recent years.

->Some states have a higher EV presence than others, indicating policy influences and market demand.

->CAFV eligibility plays a crucial role in the adoption of clean energy vehicles.
